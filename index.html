Touch CAD

A parametric CAD made for fingers and styluses. Keyboard and mouse work too, but are never required.

  • One finger or the stylus: draw, tap to select, drag to move.
  • Two fingers: move together to rotate, pinch to zoom (and pan). In a sketch, two fingers pan and zoom.
  • Two-finger tap: undo. Three-finger tap: redo.
  • Back gesture: cancel / leave the sketch.

Workflow: New sketch → pick a plane → draw → select geometry and use the constraints in the right panel → Finish → Extrude.

Values are typed on the keypad, which accepts expressions like 20*2+3. An extrusion can also be pulled by its handle.
